ArchiCalc Beta 1.3.1

Architectural & Standard Precision Calculator

⚠️
Note: This is a beta release currently in the testing stage. Functionality and accuracy are still being evaluated. Feedback and bug reports are welcome.

ArchiCalc is designed specifically for architects, builders, and planners to perform precise dimensional calculations. It supports entry and manipulation of measurements in feet, inches, and fractions — and seamlessly converts between imperial and metric units.

Dual Architectural & Regular Modes Tap the toggle switch in the bottom right corner to switch instantly between the Architectural Calculator and a Standard Calculator.

Install on Apple & Android Phones

Apple iPhone & iPad
  1. Open in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (box with arrow).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
Android Phones
  1. Tap browser menu (⋮).
  2. Tap Install App or Add to Home screen.

Key Features

  • Dimensional Input: Work directly in feet, inches, and fractions (e.g., 5 ft 6 3/8 in).
  • Measurement Modes: Compute linear, area (sq ft / sq in), and volume (cu ft / cu in).
  • SQ-CU Mode: Toggle to enter direct area (SQ) or volume (CU) measurements.
  • Metric Conversion: Seamless conversion between imperial and metric (meters, cm, mm).
  • Fraction Rounding: Press round () repeatedly on results to cycle to nearest fraction (1/32, 1/16, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 1").
  • Calculation Tape: Scrollable tape keeps full history. Tap any tape entry to copy.
